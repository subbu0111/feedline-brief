Feedline 2.5.0 · final

This is an AI agent

Feedline lives in Chrome. It does not log into LinkedIn and does not post for you. At 8:00 AM IST it searches the public index of LinkedIn posts, ranks the top 5, drafts practitioner comments with Gemini, and publishes this phone page.

Pipeline

1
Trigger 8:00 AM IST Chrome alarm, or Run search in the side panel. Laptop may be locked. Chrome must stay open.
↓
2
Discover · public index Bing + DuckDuckGo. Google only if it is not rate-limited (429). Queries: agentic AI, agent harness, multi-agent, GenAI production, AI governance, pilot to production, operating model, Infosys Topaz.
↓
3
Window Last 2 hours first. If fewer than 5, 6 hours. Then 24 hours. Nothing older.
↓
4
Rank Interaction (comments + reactions) + substance. Keep only the top 5.
↓
5
Write Gemini 2.5 Flash, then Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite. No Nemotron. Topic restates this post. Comments: no “Warm reply:” label. At most 1–2 of 5 end with a question.
↓
6
Publish GitHub docs/index.html → phone page. You copy the comment and paste on LinkedIn yourself.

What it is not

  • Not a LinkedIn login or auto-comment bot
  • Not a Home-feed scroller
  • Not ChatGPT. The model is Gemini via OpenRouter
